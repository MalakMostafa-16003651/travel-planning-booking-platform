## 9. 2026-11-07 — fix: PAY-01 payment-success race and current-policy check

- **Branch:** `fix/PAY-01-payment-race-and-policy-check` from `develop` `30f5ac2` (all six Person 8 PRs merged). Requirements: PAY-01, PAY-02.
- **Source:** the team leader's 2026-11-07 review in `16003651.md` ("payment-success race (P1)" and "current-policy acceptance bypass (P2)"), each reproduced there with a probe against the compiled services. The student asked for every remaining item inside Person 8's own modules to be fixed as new PRs.
- **Race (P1):** after a retry replaced intent A with B, two concurrent success handlers for A could both read the payment as PENDING with B. The loser's conditional update failed and, trusting its stale read, refunded A although A had won and confirmed the booking. The same stale read also meant that when A and B both succeeded at once, the losing charge could be left unrefunded. **Fix:** when the conditional update fails, the handler re-reads the stored payment and decides from it: the same intent won → finish it (no refund); another intent won → refund this one as a second charge.
- **Policy bypass (P2):** `POST /api/payments` returned an existing PENDING payment before checking the current policy acceptance, and retry never checked it, so a provider policy change after the first attempt did not require a new acceptance. **Fix:** both paths now require the current policies to be accepted (409 otherwise). A SUCCEEDED/REFUNDED/FAILED payment still answers its existing 409 first.
- **Tests:** three new cases in `payments.e2e.test.ts`, using a barrier that makes two handlers act on the same earlier read (the review's interleaving): two deliveries of the older intent (no refund, booking confirmed); older and newer intents succeeding at once (exactly one full refund of the loser); policy change before reuse and before retry (409 until accepted again). All three **FAIL on the previous code and PASS with the fix** (checked by running them against `develop`'s version of `payments.service.ts`).
- **Checks:** `npm run build` PASS; `payments.e2e.test.ts` 25/25 PASS; full suite recorded in the PR.

## 10. 2026-11-08 — fix: blog counter and post-deletion races

- **Branch:** `fix/BLG-06-counter-and-deletion-races` from `develop` `c49ecda`. Requirements: BLG-04, BLG-05, BLG-06, BLG-09, BLG-10.

- **Counter race:** a like or comment is saved before the post's counter is raised. An unlike or comment deletion in between skipped its decrement (it only ran while the counter was above 0), so the later increment left a count of 1 with no rows. **Fix:** decrements are unconditional, so every removed row cancels exactly its own increment in any order; the like response never shows a negative count.
- **Deletion race:** deleting a post removed its images, comments and likes before the post, so an upload in between left an orphaned image, and a like or comment that had passed the "post is public" check could survive its post. **Fix:** the post is deleted first, then its children; a like, comment or image saved after the post is gone finds no post, removes itself and answers 404.
